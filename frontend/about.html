About ZinoEx

ZinoEx is a legal-tech platform designed to support legal professionals and businesses in drafting, reviewing, and managing contracts more efficiently.

By combining AI-assisted analysis with structured legal logic, ZinoEx supports contract-related decision-making through practical use cases such as contract drafting, contract review, and document integrity verification. The platform places particular emphasis on transparency, risk awareness, and compliance-oriented design.

ZinoEx is built to assist decision-making, reduce friction in routine legal tasks, and improve access to structured legal insights — while keeping legal responsibility where it belongs: with the user and their legal counsel.

ZinoEx also offers an AI Risk Assessment module that supports organizations in identifying and documenting potential legal, ethical, and operational risks related to the use of AI systems, in line with emerging governance and responsible AI principles.
