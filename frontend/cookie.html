What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve user experience, and provide information about how the site is used.

Cookies may be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (stored on your device for a longer period).

These cookies are necessary for the basic operation of the Platform. They enable core functionalities such as security, session management, and access to protected areas.

This information is used only to improve functionality and user experience and does not directly identify individual users.

These cookies allow the Platform to remember certain user preferences and settings, such as language or session choices, to provide a more personalized experience.
